Privacy Policy

Flow • Developed by PlayMontage • Effective Date: October 2026 (Version 1.1+)

Summary: Flow is a top-down arcade traffic control game. We do not require any accounts, passwords, or personal registrations. To support free gameplay, Flow integrates Google AdMob for optional rewarded video ads. We do not sell or store personal identifying data on proprietary servers.

1. Information Collection and Usage

Flow does not collect, log, or transmit any Personally Identifiable Information (such as your real name, home address, email address, or phone number) directly to our own servers. You can enjoy the full game without creating an account or signing in.

2. Third-Party Advertising and Analytics (Google AdMob)

Flow displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob (a service provided by Google LLC). AdMob helps us monetize the game and offer features like optional Rewarded Ads (where players can choose to watch an ad in exchange for an in-game revive).

To serve, personalize, and measure advertisements, Google AdMob and its partners may automatically collect certain device information when you interact with ads in the app, including:

  • Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID on Android) or Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA on iOS).
  • Device and Network Information: Device model, operating system version, carrier, network connection type, and general coarse location derived from IP address.
  • Ad Performance Metrics: Diagnostic data, impressions, clicks, video completion rates, and crash reports.

Google’s handling of collected data is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy. For detailed information on how Google collects and processes data, please visit:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services
  • Google Privacy Policy

3. Rewarded Video Ads Policy

Flow provides optional Rewarded Video Advertisements. In accordance with Google AdMob’s Policies for Ads that Offer Rewards:

  • Rewarded ads are strictly opt-in. Players must affirmatively tap an explicit prompt (such as "WATCH AD TO REVIVE") to initiate an ad.
  • The reward granted (a single in-game retry/revive) is consumed strictly within the current level session and holds no real-world monetary value.
  • Players are never forced or misled into viewing rewarded advertisements.

4. User Rights and Ad Personalization Controls

You have full control over interest-based advertising on your mobile device:

  • On Android: Open your device Settings > Google > Ads (or Privacy > Ads), where you can opt out of Ads Personalization or reset/delete your Advertising ID.
  • On iOS: Open Settings > Privacy > Tracking or Settings > Privacy > Apple Advertising to toggle tracking permissions.

Opting out will not stop ads from appearing, but the ads served will be contextual rather than tailored to your interests.

5. Local Device Storage

Flow stores gameplay progress strictly on your device using standard local storage (such as SharedPreferences). This includes:

  • High scores, level star achievements, and unlocked map progression.
  • Audio and sound effect mute preferences.
  • Tutorial completion status.

This data remains on your physical device. It is never transmitted to external cloud servers and will be cleared if you choose to uninstall the application or clear app data.

6. Device Permissions

Flow is designed to be lightweight and respectful of your privacy. The application requests minimal, standard permissions:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required strictly to load AdMob advertisements.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required by the AdMob SDK to determine whether an active network connection exists before attempting to load an ad.

Flow does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files, or precise GPS location.

7. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Flow is intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly solicit, collect, or store personal information from children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the European Union). Furthermore, our ad requests are configured in compliance with Google Play’s Families Policy and age-neutral requirements.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational, legal, or regulatory updates. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated Effective Date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
