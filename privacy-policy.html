Privacy Policy

Flow • Developed by Play Montage • Effective Date: October 2026
Summary: Flow is an offline arcade game. We do not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data, device identifiers, or tracking metrics.

1. Information Collection and Use

Flow is designed as a standalone offline single-player arcade game. We do not require account creation, registration, or sign-ins. We do not collect any Personally Identifiable Information (PII), location information, or device hardware IDs.

2. Permissions

Flow requires no invasive device permissions. The application does not request or access:

  • Precise or approximate location
  • Camera or microphone
  • Contacts or phone state
  • External storage files

3. Local Storage & Saved Progress

Your high scores, star ratings, unlocked maps, and audio preferences are saved locally on your device via standard local app storage. This data never leaves your device and is erased if you choose to uninstall or clear app data.

4. Third-Party Services & Analytics

Flow v1 does not integrate third-party advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, behavioral analytics, or third-party data processors.

5. Children’s Privacy

Flow does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children or any other users. Because no personal data is collected at all, Flow is compliant with global privacy standards, including COPPA and GDPR.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted directly on this page with an updated effective date.
